Co-Founder & Executive Director

Greta McAnany

Greta is a public health entrepreneur focused on youth mental health through media and tech. A former award-winning filmmaker, she brings over a decade of experience in youth care, blending storytelling, product, and civic leadership.

As CEO and co-founder of Blue Fever—a mental health AI companion for Gen Z/Alpha—she's supported 3M+ youth, earning recognition from RockHealth, Forbes, and Apple.

A 2024 RockHealth Innovator and Pivotal Ventures grantee, Greta advises on youth trust, safety, and data privacy policy in California and New Mexico. Her work appears in TechCrunch, CBS, NBC, Nylon, and Masters of Scale.

Co-Founder & Executive Producer

Dani Bicknell

Dani is an author, educator, and cross-sector collaborator dedicated to bringing clarity, integrity, and cohesion to the digital mental health ecosystem. With more than a decade of experience across sectors, she has designed accelerator programs, frameworks, and research initiatives for UNESCO, UC Berkeley, global NGOs, large tech companies, and early-stage social impact ventures worldwide.

During the pandemic, Dani led accelerator and funder research that surfaced a defining insight: meaningful innovation emerges when founders, funders, and system enablers align around evidence, equity, and lived experience.

Dani co-founded MCert to establish the non-partisan Quality Standards that foster Systemic Trust, unlock responsible innovation, and benefit everyone seeking clarity in this complex market.
